# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the OntoPilot community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not only for us as individuals.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's [GitHub Security Advisory form](https://github.com/deeplethe/ontopilot/security/advisories/new). Do not include sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to investigate the report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, fairly, and confidentially. They will protect the privacy and safety of reporters and affected community members as far as reasonably possible. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.

Proportionate responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent ban, depending on impact and repeated behavior. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
